# Teach me

A five-word reason ("accent on past tense") fixes this message. Understanding the
rule fixes the next fifty. **Teach me** turns one change's explanation into a
one-minute lesson for that exact decision, in the writer's own language.

## The moment

The writer has tapped a change on the check card, read the short reason, and
thinks "I keep getting this wrong". Or, later, they are looking back at a saved
check in History. Either way they have a minute and a specific question: what is
the rule here, and why was my version wrong?

It does not get in the way of sending. The link sits under the explanation, after
Accept, and nothing loads until it is tapped.

## How to use it

1. Check text as usual (selection menu, accessibility button or Try it).
2. Tap an underlined change to open its explanation.
3. Tap **Teach me**. The lesson opens in place, inside the card:
   - **the rule**, in two or three plain sentences;
   - **your version**: why it was wrong, or correct but less natural, in this sentence;
   - **examples**: three short sentences in the target language, with translations;
   - **common trap**: the mistake learners make with this rule, and how to catch it;
   - **quick quiz**: two multiple-choice questions. The first tap on an option
     answers it: it shows Right or Not quite, marks the right answer, and gives
     one line of feedback. After both, the score and **Try again**.
4. **Hide lesson** folds it back into the link.

The same link is under each explanation in a saved check (History, open a check,
then open a change under Suggestions).

## How it works

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| Topic, lesson and quiz state (pure) | `core/teachme/Lesson.kt`, `core/teachme/Quiz.kt` |
| Prompt and schema assets | `app/src/main/assets/lab_teach_me_prompt.md`, `lab_teach_me_schema.json` |
| Request and strict parsing | `llm/teachme/LessonPrompt.kt` |
| Lab-local cache | `data/teachme/LessonStore.kt` (`files/lab_teach_me.json`) |
| Orchestration | `Teacher.kt` (created in `App`) |
| UI and state machine | `ui/teachme/TeachMe.kt`, `LessonController.kt`, `LessonTopics.kt` |

- **Topic.** A change's `from`, `to` and `why`, plus the sentence it sits in. On the
  live card the sentence comes from the suggested text. In History it comes from the
  saved text, and only when the saved span still holds the saved words. History also
  gives the check's language; the live card lets the model name it.
- **Request.** `Teacher` uses the provider, model and key chosen in Settings, through
  the existing provider clients' generic `generate` path, with its own system prompt
  and JSON schema. The user message is JSON data (`explainIn`, `language`, `from`,
  `to`, `why`, `sentence`); the writer's language comes from the app, never from the
  text. The check prompt and schema are untouched.
- **Parsing.** `LessonPrompt.read` accepts exactly three examples and two questions,
  two to four distinct options per question, a `correct` index in range, no empty or
  oversized fields and no unknown fields. Anything else is an unusable reply, shown
  with the app's usual failure titles and a Retry button.
- **Cache.** Lessons are saved in `lab_teach_me.json` in Lab's private files, keyed by
  target language + from + to + why + the writer's language (spacing collapsed,
  languages compared without case). A lesson is also saved under the language the
  model named, so the card (which may not know the language) and History (which does)
  share it. Opening a lesson checks the cache first, so a repeat mistake opens at once,
  offline and without a key. The writer's sentence is never stored. The newest 200
  lessons are kept.
- **Quiz state.** `QuizState` is a pure value: the first choice for a question locks
  it, `score` and `finished` follow from the choices, `reset` starts over.
  `LessonController` moves between Closed, Loading, Ready and Failed, and drops a
  lesson that arrives after the writer hid it.

## Limits

- A lesson is generated by the model the writer picked. It can be wrong, like any
  reason on the card. There is no eval for lessons yet.
- Lessons are not linked to History records. Deleting History does not delete saved
  lessons. They hold only the change, its reason and the lesson, and are removed
  with Lab's app data.
- Spacing-only changes (a space or line break) offer no lesson.
- The cache matches a decision by its exact reason text. The same rule with a
  differently worded reason makes a new lesson.
- One language per lesson: explanations follow the UI language (`native_language`).
