Linguize language catalog: third-party notices

lab_languages.json and the res/drawable-nodpi/lab_flag_*.webp images are built from the
two works below. See docs/lab/language-catalog.md. The emblems drawn for the ten languages
without a flag (res/drawable/lab_emblem_*.xml) are Linguize's own drawings.


1. circle-flags by HatScripts
   https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags (commit 379588b)
   The SVG flags are rasterised to 96x96 px lossless WebP images, clipped to a circle.
